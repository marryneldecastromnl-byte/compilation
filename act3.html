Kare Kare Pata

Kare-kare is one of the classic dish of Filipino Cuisine

About Ingredients Procedure

About This Recipe

Kare-kare is a traditional Filipino stew known for its rich, savory peanut-based sauce and colorful mix of vegetables. It is one of the most popular Filipino dishes and is usually served with bagoong.


Ingredients

  • Whole pork pata (about 1.2 kg), cut into serving pieces
  • Peanut butter or ground roasted peanuts
  • Annatto (atsuete) for color
  • Roasted rice flour (or ground toasted rice) for thickening
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Beef or pork broth (or the broth from boiling the pata)
  • Eggplant
  • String beans (sitaw)
  • Bok choy (pechay)
  • Fish sauce, salt, and black pepper
  • Bagoong alamang (shrimp paste), for serving

Cooking Procedure

  1. Boil the pork pata in water with onion and garlic until tender. Reserve the broth.
  2. Prepare the vegetables by washing and cutting them into serving pieces. Cook them separately until just tender, then set aside.
  3. Make the sauce by bringing the reserved broth to a simmer. Add annatto water, ground peanuts or peanut butter, and toasted rice flour.
  4. Add the cooked pork pata to the sauce and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes.
  5. Season with fish sauce (optional), salt, and black pepper to taste.
  6. Add the vegetables just before serving to keep them crisp and colorful.
  7. Serve hot with steamed white rice and bagoong alamang (shrimp paste) on the side.
